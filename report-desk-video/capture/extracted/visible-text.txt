Report Desk — product film brief (no-capture mode; no website exists to crawl).

WHAT IT IS
Report Desk is an AI agent that writes a marketing agency's monthly client
reports. It pulls each client's figures, writes the summary, then checks every
number it just wrote against the real source data. If a figure is wrong it
rewrites that part and checks again. Anything unusual it flags for a human to
explain. Nothing reaches a client until someone reads it and clicks approve.

WHO IT IS FOR
Owners of 5-50 person marketing and SEO agencies in the UK and US.

THE ONE MESSAGE
You can trust it, because it catches its own mistakes and never sends without you.

BEATS
1. The pain — a roster of 20 clients; monthly reports eat days.
2. Report Desk drafts all of them at once.
3. HERO — it catches its own wrong number and rewrites it (the fact-check loop).
4. Stat drop — "41 figures checked", "6 corrected before you saw them".
5. A human approves; a branded client report goes out.
6. End card.
